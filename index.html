Sumo Rings

Shove everyone off the ring before it shrinks away.

Everyone wears a bumper ring. Run into other players to shove them: the faster you hit, the harder they fly. The platform shrinks as the round goes on. Fall off and you are out, and the last one standing wins.

Play it

Sumo Rings is a whole room, not a game you add to your own world. Open it as a space in VR or on desktop:

  • Altspace: type sumo.firer.at into the search bar.
  • Banter: open https://sumo.firer.at as a space.

The game runs entirely in the space, so there is no server and nothing to install.

How to play

  1. Stand on the green pad in the lobby. The game starts when two or more players are on it.
  2. Run into other players to shove them. The faster you hit, the harder they fly.
  3. The ring shrinks over time, so there is less and less room.
  4. Fall off and you are out.
  5. The last one standing wins.

Controls

The pads and the control panel are in the lobby.

  • Orange pad: practise alone against the Sumo Bot.
  • Sound: turn the game sounds on or off, just for you.
  • Reset game: end the game and go back to the lobby.
